# Intro Video Script

**Duration:** 30–45 seconds  
**Tone:** Confident, clear, professional, trustworthy  
**Speaker:** Maurice Nobles, FASS Systems Consulting

---

## Script

Hi, I'm Maurice Nobles with FASS Systems Consulting.

I help lenders, real estate professionals, insurance companies, and growing service businesses improve their operations.

If your team is struggling with scattered workflows, inconsistent follow-up, or unclear reporting—I can help you fix that.

My background is in mortgage operations, underwriting, and technical systems. I've spent years bridging the gap between strategy and hands-on execution.

What makes me different? I don't just give advice—I help you build practical systems your team will actually use.

If you're ready to bring more structure, visibility, and accountability to your business, let's connect.

Reach out here on LinkedIn. I'd be happy to talk.

---

## Notes

- Deliver with steady pacing—not rushed, not slow
- Maintain direct eye contact with camera
- Professional but approachable demeanor
- Consider recording in a clean, well-lit space with minimal background distractions
- Dress business casual or business professional

## Key Points to Emphasize

1. **Who I am** — Maurice Nobles, FASS Systems Consulting
2. **Who I help** — Lenders, real estate, insurance, service businesses
3. **What I improve** — Operations, workflows, reporting, execution
4. **What makes me different** — Hands-on implementation, not just advice
5. **Call to action** — Connect on LinkedIn

## Alternative Closing Options

**Option A (Standard):**
> "Reach out here on LinkedIn. I'd be happy to talk."

**Option B (Offer-focused):**
> "If you want to start with a systems audit, send me a message. Let's find out what's slowing you down."

**Option C (Soft ask):**
> "Follow along for more insights on operations and workflow—and reach out when you're ready to make improvements."
